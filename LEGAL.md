The current scaffold links to the SRD but **does not import its rules text**. When adapted rules are added, identify modifications and maintain attribution and notices consistent with the license. “5E-compatible” does not mean endorsed.

## Elder Scrolls setting
Names, setting and protected material in The Elder Scrolls are not licensed by the SRD or by CC BY 4.0. Do not copy/redistribute games, dialogue collections, books, maps, character art, fan-wiki prose, or other protected assets. Use concise **original** lore descriptions, dates, primary-source citations/links and original rules conversions. Avoid importing assets from game files. Attribute factual references where possible.

No blanket permission is asserted. Reassess legal questions before expanding distribution, packaging copyrighted content, or monetizing.

## Original contributions
No blanket repository-wide software/content license has been chosen yet. Until one is intentionally adopted, do not assert that original Elf-Books content is open-source under the SRD license.
