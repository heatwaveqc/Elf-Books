# Character creation — Geard Lif edition (not yet executable)

Character creation cannot begin in a persistent live solo game until upstream Geard trait/roll numbers and Elf-Books' skill mapping are approved.

1. Choose historical era, initial region, canon continuity, and character concept.
2. Choose ancestry/culture/background and optional archetype. No classes or character levels.
3. Record all **12 independent attributes** (**Elf-Books expanded rating 5–25 at creation**, each contributes floor(rating/5) dice; **do not substitute core Geard Lif's 0–5 values/Drive Marks**): Body (Strength, Dexterity, Agility, Health), Mind (Intelligence, Perception, Wits, Will), Soul (Charisma, Insight, Temperance, Conviction). Resilience mark slots use floor(Health/Will/Conviction / 5); the starting budget beyond these minimums remains open.
4. Choose **exactly one Talent** from [the twelve presets or a custom named five-skill package](../rules/TALENTS.md). **Set every one of its five governed Skills to at least rating 5 before allocating any optional extra starting Skill points**; each now supplies at least one die. Other Skills and Specialties may start at zero. All 24 remain trainable regardless of Talent, and only the five governed Skills receive Talent dice. Talent rating and total allocation budget remain to be chosen.
5. Set equipment, starting Effects/Tiers, spell schools and legal resource Costs. Do not reuse D20 HP, MP, AC, hit bonus, or 0–100 skill ratings.
6. Record the [Elf-Books-specific 10×-rating XP curve and source-die multipliers](../rules/ADVANCEMENT.md), which intentionally replaces the base Geard Lif Drive Mark advancement model. Skill and Specialty bootstrap on each natural 10, including simultaneously where both are below rating 5. Passive-defense XP, Talent-tree progression and bankable critical perks are still open.
7. Confirm personal hooks, danger/lethality preferences, style of play and boundaries.
8. Approve a complete public sheet, then conduct [private pre-session work](PRE_SESSION_PREP.md) before opening the first scene.

Never fill unimplemented mechanics with unsupported numbers or start a game simply because the template is syntactically valid. See [P0 prerequisites](../MIGRATION_BACKLOG.md).

**Tier advancement:** All new traits start at **Tier 0**. After a trait reaches rating 25 at one Tier, the character may buy its next Tier at ×10 cost; using higher-Tier Skill/Specialty dice requires at least one corresponding Attribute/Skill die at that same Tier. Higher-rated children are allowed. See [Advancement](../rules/ADVANCEMENT.md). No initial Tier 1 budget has been approved.

**Resource tracks:** Initialize **Mind (Will)** with Spirit (slow) and Mana (fast), **Body (Health)** with Injury (slow) and Fatigue (fast), **Soul (Conviction)** with Plasticity (slow) and Attention (fast). A mage may improve the *Mana Cost severity threshold* using applicable Talent/School/Spell Specialty and cost-reducing equipment, but only Will determines Mind mark slots.

**Social durability:** Soul/Conviction is the capacity/soak for Attention and Plasticity but does not replace discernment or composure defensive pools. Ordinary social influences threaten Attention only; Plasticity requires supernatural intrusion. Up-tier advancement and action magnitude use the current **×5 per Tier** rule.
