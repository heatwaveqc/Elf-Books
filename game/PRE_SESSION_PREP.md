# Mandatory GM pre-session preparation

**Hard gate** between character approval and the opening scene. Derived as an original adaptation of situation-based prep principles from the Star Wars Solo workflow, Justin Alexander's “Don't Prep Plots” / Three Clue Rule, and Sly Flourish's lightweight GM prep. Prep *causal situations* rather than mandatory PC actions.

## Player-approved public commitments
Record era/date/region and continuity strictness; character and at least one approved personal hook; desired genre/tone; combat vs social vs exploration emphasis; challenge/lethality; content boundaries; advancement method; agreed mechanics; private save location and its access limits. If rules are pending, **do not activate**.

## Private campaign spine (must be concretely filled)
1. **True situation:** what already happened, when, what is believed, what is false, and why the PC is involved. No anachronisms without a declared divergence.
2. **Central conflict:** stakes, what happens if the player walks away, an underlying question and plausible trajectories, not a predetermined script.
3. **Opposition:** at least one responsible antagonist/faction decision-maker with goal, resources, limits, incomplete knowledge, independent agency and three conditional escalation actions.
4. **Living world:** at least two factions, three consequential NPCs (at least two with their own next moves), one active clock with trigger/consequence, and credible social tensions.
5. **Two planning horizons:** at least three local reachable locations and three potential near-term problems; two broader developments or outcomes. Do not write a mandatory chapter sequence.
6. **Discoverable truths:** a key revelation supported by three independent clues across at least two different discovery methods; fixed current holders/locations; no single mandatory skill check.
7. **Real choice:** two plausible distinct responses, two credible resolutions and real consequences of delay/refusal. Don't solve problems with canon protagonists.
8. **Encounter-ready details:** 1–2 scenes with legal NPC capabilities, cues for danger, and noncombat alternatives. Solo balancing must account for the actual PC, not a 4-person D&D party.
9. **Opening:** specific incident, why now, what the PC immediately sees, two possible actions and the consequences of inaction.

## Readiness check
Both public character approval and private prep must be recorded. Set `preflight.status=ready` only if the selected rules version is *playable*, campaign data validates, and the save has actually reached private canonical storage. Templates may allow `character_creation` and `preparing` with incomplete private fields; active/paused/complete must pass readiness checks. If failure occurs, remain preparing and disclose what is missing without spoiling secrets.

## Continued play
Revisit opposition knowledge, independent clocks and near/far horizons after major events. Let the player change the world without retroactively moving clues, rewriting motives or protecting a scheduled finale.
