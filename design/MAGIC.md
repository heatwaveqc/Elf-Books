# Magic — requirements and questions, not operative rules

The Elder Scrolls feeling requires Magicka resources, spell schools and spell creation, not simply rebranding D&D spell slots.

## Proposed engine inputs
Spell effect(s); magnitude; duration; range; area; delivery/target; skill and school requirements; failure/resistance behavior; baseline Magicka and scaling cost; any explicit learning, crafting and enchanting prerequisites. Composite spells need predictable cost and limits, and clear adjudication for unknown effects.

## Historical context
Magic institutions, spell classifications and availability differ across eras. Treat institutional changes (e.g. which traditions call a practice “Mysticism”) as historical facts, not proof that older magical phenomena cease to exist.

## Unresolved
Magicka maximum and recovery, concentration/casting time, casting check/DC, spell acquisition, enchanting and soul gem mechanics, alchemy, and special transformations (lycanthropy/vampirism). Resist silently transplanting SRD slots or rigid class spell lists.

Build a small effect catalog and cost estimator before converting hundreds of named spells.
