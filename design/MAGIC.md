# Elder Scrolls magic on Geard Lif (OPEN)

Use the local [Geard Lif Project, d10 and resource rules](../engine/geard-lif/README.md). **Magicka as a fixed MP pool and D20 spell slots are retired**.

Geard's Soul aspect has **Conviction** Resilience, **Mana** (fast) and **Spirit** (slow). A spell's Cost is an *unopposed self-targeted Effect roll* that applies graded marks, not a fixed amount of points subtracted from a pool.

Retain recognizable Elder Scrolls schools: **Alteration, Conjuration, Destruction, Illusion, Mysticism, Restoration**, with **Alchemy** and **Enchanting** integrating into the universal Project economy where applicable. Define each effect's Accuracy, Potence, Tier, duration, area, control, applicability, and resource Cost once the upstream roll contract is finalized.

An Oakflesh-like effect should modify the **effective Injury-grade threshold**, not the relevant Health/Resilience-based count of available marks. Spell Cost marks are self-inflicted using their normal threshold and cannot be discounted by armor.

Immediate tests: damaging Destruction spell against Injury; Restoration recovery; Oakflesh-style Avoidance/Resilience defense; soul-trapping/enchanting; a simple summoning spell with duration and limits. None should be defined by the old per-HP/MP experience numbers. Learning through meaningful magic use remains a design goal, not a licensed excuse to spam zero-effect Costs.

[Return-to-parity checklist](../MIGRATION_BACKLOG.md) covers spell economy, Mana recovery, school progress and major magical artifacts.
