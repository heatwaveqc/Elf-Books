# Elder Scrolls perk survey — Geard Lif conversion queue

The D20-era [survey](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Elf-Books/blob/archive/d20-pre-geard-lif/design/TES_PERK_PORT_SURVEY.md) and its complete analysis are preserved in the archive. The operative research inventory is now [75 TES perk inspirations](tes_perk_seeds.json), with original game names, source links and game skill-gate references **for provenance, not as current Geard gates**.

**Straightforward thematic ports:** Quiet Casting (Illusion), Arcane Blacksmith (Smithing), Extra Effect (Enchanting), Green Thumb/Concentrated Poison (Alchemy), Light Foot (Sneak), Wax Key (Security), Poisoned (Legerdemain), Conditioning (Heavy Armor), Hunter's Discipline (Archery). They need actual Geard action/Project/material rules before purchase.

**Substantial conversions:** Sweep, Shield Charge, Twin Souls, Impact, Shadow Warrior, Slow Time/aiming and critical hit perks. These interact with Potence, Accuracy, Tier, graded resources or the still-provisional d10 critical model.

**Important change:** Geard Lif has **Fatigue**, so Skyrim Stamina perks may be appropriate once mapped; their earlier D20 rejection is no longer reliable. Likewise +percentage damage/armor mechanics can be considered as Potence/Resilience conversions instead of copied as percentages.

The 24 candidate skill labels are in [rules/skills.json](../rules/skills.json). No perk has been assigned a canonical Geard cost, prereq, dice effect or resource impact.
