# Character progression on Geard Lif — transition brief

**Status:** The old D20 0–100 XP curves, HP/Magicka ECL, and skill bonus increments are in the [archived branch](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Elf-Books/tree/archive/d20-pre-geard-lif). Elf-Books deliberately retains the 0–25 **XP-per-use Elder Scrolls adapter** in [rules/ADVANCEMENT.md](../rules/ADVANCEMENT.md) (working), **in contrast to current base Geard Lif's 0–5 ratings and Drive Marks** and [rules/PERKS.md](../rules/PERKS.md) (bankable-perk design retained, d10 triggers open).

Characters remain classless; optional archetypes are starting descriptions, never irreversible skill gates. Relative capability uses upstream Geard's Attribute/Talent/Skill/Specialty dice contributions and potentially independent Tiers, both requiring finalization before starting characters can be valid. Learning-by-doing through legitimate combat, spellcasting, crafting, social work and study is the goal.

**Next milestone:** define skill-rating scale, conversion of genuine Project progress into practice improvement, passive defensive practice, magical resource costs and critical-derived perk points. Run equal-skill and novice/expert comparisons across the four vertical-slice test cases.
