# Character progression on Geard Lif — transition brief

**Status:** The old D20 0–100 XP curves, HP/Magicka ECL, and skill bonus increments are in the [archived branch](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Elf-Books/tree/archive/d20-pre-geard-lif). Elf-Books deliberately retains the 0–25 **XP-per-use Elder Scrolls adapter** in [rules/ADVANCEMENT.md](../rules/ADVANCEMENT.md) (working), **in contrast to current base Geard Lif's 0–5 ratings and Drive Marks** and [rules/PERKS.md](../rules/PERKS.md) (bankable perks and personal-only double-10/defender double-1 confirmations adopted).

Characters remain classless; optional archetypes are starting descriptions, never irreversible skill gates. Relative capability uses upstream Geard's Attribute/Talent/Skill/Specialty dice contributions and potentially independent Tiers, both requiring finalization before starting characters can be valid. Learning-by-doing through legitimate combat, spellcasting, crafting, social work and study is the goal.

**Next milestone:** author actual perk effects, attribute/specialty gates, supernatural condition perk trees for vampirism/lycanthropy, and playtest fast bankable point gain. The common perk-point trigger and 0–100 composite gate are adopted; equipment die confirmation as future deed enchantment remains deliberately unimplemented. Run novice/expert and four-scene comparisons.
