# Elf-Books foundation — Geard Lif / Raven's Heart

The **local Geard Lif snapshot** at [engine/geard-lif/](../engine/geard-lif/README.md) provides the mechanical base. Its source rules are not D20-derived. This file describes the Elder Scrolls overlay, not a second conflicting resolution system.

## Fundamental resolution (upstream)

Every consequential action, **including combat**, is a Project characterized by **Complexity, Recalcitrance, Scope, Time**. Accuracy applies against static Complexity/Avoidance, then Potence expresses the Effect against Recalcitrance/Resilience. Excess Accuracy does **not** automatically increase Potence. **Meet it to beat it:** Accuracy successes **>=** Complexity/Avoidance apply; then roll Potence independently. Potence zero yields no mark, positive below effective threshold Minor, at R Middling, 2R Major, 3R Final, with further multiples adding Final marks. **Project Scope equals slots at each of four grades**, Recalcitrance controls the threshold; **personal Resilience equals slots per grade**, though armor and equivalents can raise the injury threshold without changing capacity. These are *mark grades*, not cross-scale Tiers.

Personal aspects are **Mind/Body/Soul** with Resilience **Will/Health/Conviction**. Each has fast and slow graded resources: Attention/Plasticity; Fatigue/Injury; Mana/Spirit. They use Minor/Middling/Major/Final marks and recovery. A Cost is a self-targeted Effect, not a fixed amount of Magicka deducted.

The current **provisional** randomizer is a Storyteller-style d10 pool (7–9 = 1 success, 10 = 2); Tier conversion is also provisional (roughly 10× per Tier). See the local source docs before adjudicating.

## Elf-Books adaptation boundaries

- **24 familiar skills** are candidate identities and activities, not guaranteed 0–100 ratings. Geard's Attribute/Talent/Skill/Specialty mapping is open.
- **Classless** Elder Scrolls characters retain race/culture/history and personal choices. ECL-based HP/MP and D20 ability-score bonuses are deprecated.
- **Perks and use-based growth** stay design goals, but natural 20/natural 1 and per-HP/per-MP numerical awards are archived and need a d10/graded-resource version.
- **Items/spells/abilities** use Geard Accuracy/Potence, Avoidance/Resilience, resource Costs and Project constraints. No D20 AC or spell slots.
- **Solo narrative compression** is allowed; an entire week-long smithing Project may fit in one response, but Time, Costs and independent clocks are never elided.
