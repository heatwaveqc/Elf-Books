# Historical campaign entrypoints (index placeholders)

**Index only:** these are example selections, not a fully populated or verified history database.

- **Merethic Era:** mythic/legendary accounts require explicit ambiguity handling.
- **First Era:** early kingdoms, empires and regional conflicts; choose region and a more precise date before preparing NPCs.
- **Second Era:** Interregnum and competing polities in many periods; avoid treating all years as identical.
- **Third Era:** Septim period, with substantial changes over time and place.
- **Fourth Era:** post-Oblivion Crisis, but its politics and available factions depend heavily on the selected year.

For each campaign generate an `era_snapshot` with date precision, current rulership claims, active factions, recent events, local conflicts, local travel options and major anachronism checks. Source specific facts before relying on them. Player-approved alternate histories branch from a known snapshot.
